# OctoAcme Project Management Roles and Personas

This document describes the key roles and personas involved in project management at OctoAcme, including their responsibilities and interactions.

---

## Existing Roles

### Project Manager
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Oversee project planning, execution, delivery, and closure.
  - Manage resources, timelines, and budgets.
  - Facilitate communication among stakeholders.

### Team Lead
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Direct day-to-day technical work.
  - Mentor and support team members.
  - Coordinate with the Project Manager and other leads.

### Developer
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Implement project requirements.
  - Participate in code reviews and testing.
  - Provide input on design and feasibility.

---

## New Roles / Personas

### Product Owner
- **Description:**  
  Represents the voice of the customer and business, owning the vision for the product and prioritizing the work needed to maximize value delivery.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Define product goals and success metrics.
  - Maintain and prioritize the product backlog.
  - Clarify requirements for the development team.
  - Accept or reject deliverables based on business value.
- **Interactions:**  
  - Works closely with the Project Manager to align priorities and timelines.
  - Collaborates daily with Developers and Team Leads to clarify scope and unblock progress.
  - Communicates with stakeholders about priorities and value delivered.

---

### Business Analyst
- **Description:**  
  Bridges the gap between business needs and technical execution by eliciting, analyzing, and documenting requirements.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Gather and analyze stakeholder requirements.
  - Document use cases, process flows, and acceptance criteria.
  - Support communication between business and technical teams.
  - Assist in scope clarification and validation.
- **Interactions:**  
  - Partners with the Product Owner to understand business needs.
  - Collaborates with Developers to translate requirements into solutions.
  - Supports the Project Manager in requirement traceability and scope management.

---

### Quality Assurance Lead
- **Description:**  
  Ensures that deliverables meet quality standards and that risks to product quality are managed throughout the lifecycle.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Develop and own the project’s test strategy.
  - Facilitate the creation and execution of test plans and cases.
  - Coordinate bug triage and sign-off on deliverables.
  - Drive continuous improvement for QA processes.
- **Interactions:**  
  - Works with Developers and Team Leads to support test automation and best practices.
  - Reports quality metrics and concerns to the Project Manager.
  - Collaborates with the Product Owner for acceptance criteria clarity and validation.

---

### Change Manager
- **Description:**  
  Manages communication, stakeholder engagement, and process for any changes introduced to projects, minimizing disruption and resistance.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Assess project change impacts and prepare mitigation strategies.
  - Develop and execute communication and training plans.
  - Oversee implementation of approved changes.
  - Collect feedback and refine change approach.
- **Interactions:**  
  - Partners with the Project Manager to coordinate change efforts.
  - Ensures all affected team members and stakeholders are kept informed and engaged.
  - Collaborates with PMO (if applicable) for alignment on standard practices.

---

### PMO Representative
- **Description:**  
  Serves as the link to the Program/Project Management Office, ensuring adherence to organizational standards, processes, and reporting requirements.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Provide best practices, templates, and guidance.
  - Support project governance and compliance.
  - Monitor project status and escalate issues as needed.
- **Interactions:**  
  - Works with the Project Manager to ensure alignment with PMO policies.
  - Advises on methodology, risk management, and other delivery concerns.
  - Helps coordinate project audits and reviews.

---

## Interaction Overview

- The Product Owner and Business Analyst collaborate closely to ensure requirements meet business needs.
- The Project Manager relies on the Product Owner, Business Analyst, and Change Manager to ensure scope, requirements, and change impacts are well handled.
- The QA Lead works across all roles for early and continuous quality involvement.
- The PMO Representative supports the project team with governance, best practices, and reporting.
